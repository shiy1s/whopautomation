Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Whop Automation handles information when an account owner authorizes the service to support an approved video workflow.

Information we access

With your authorization through Google OAuth, the service may access Google account information needed to identify the authorized YouTube channel, YouTube video data needed for an upload or analytics workflow, and approved Google Drive file metadata or media selected for processing. We may also receive your email address and OAuth authorization details from Google.

How we use information

We use this information only to authenticate the account, acquire approved media, apply campaign rules, prepare review artifacts, perform authorized YouTube operations, and maintain execution and safety records. We do not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it to build advertising profiles.

Storage and retention

Workflow records may include account identifiers, source URLs, media hashes, campaign metadata, execution logs, and publication status. Access tokens and refresh tokens are stored only in the configured automation credential store. Records are retained only as long as needed for the workflow, audit trail, dispute handling, or legal obligations, then deleted or de-identified where practical.

Sharing

Information may be processed by the services that the account owner configured for the workflow, such as Google, n8n, GitHub Actions, and Vercel. We do not sell or rent personal information. We do not share data with unrelated third parties.

Security

OAuth credentials are handled through configured secret stores and are not placed in source code. No online system can guarantee absolute security; report suspected unauthorized access promptly.

Your choices

You can revoke Google access from your Google Account security settings and request deletion of workflow records by contacting the developer. Revoking access may stop workflows that require the authorization.
